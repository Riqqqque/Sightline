# Changelog

## 0.2.0

First public release.

- Lobby and match list for every mode: agent, name, rank badge, RR and peak, level, party.
  Deathmatch is one list; team modes are two. Players you can read come first, hidden ones last.
- Recent form per player: last five ranked results as W and L, KD, headshot rate and ADR over
  the last ten ranked matches.
- Seen before, with the record of those matches, and a note per player.
- Smurf flag, rank gap and win estimate.
- Your record on the current map from your whole ranked history, and the round score while a
  match is on.
- After the match: the full scoreboard, your first kills, first deaths and clutches, the RR
  change and your session.
- Settings screen, including a clear message when the stats key is missing.
- One-click updates from this repository's releases, checked against a published SHA-256.
- The look follows VALORANT's own scoreboard, with agent and rank artwork.

Players who hide their name stay hidden while a match is live and are never looked up. After
the match they stay nameless on the scoreboard unless "Names after the match" is turned on.
